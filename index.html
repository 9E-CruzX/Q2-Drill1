Beginner's Guide to Volleyball!!!

Learn the basic fundamentals. Play with passion!

Basic Rules

  • Six players ONLY per team
  • Maximum of three touches ONLY
  • Do not catch or hold the ball
  • Rotate after winning the serve (Three Times)

Equipment

  • Volleyball
  • Net and court
  • Volleyball shoes
  • Knee pads

Basic Techniques

  • Serving
  • Bumping and passing
  • Setting and spiking
  • Blocking
